## Instructions for Validators

Thank you for helping validate the Egyptian Arabic TTS system! This checklist will guide you through evaluating the pronunciation quality and accuracy of the generated audio.

### How to Use This Checklist

1. **Listen to each audio file** in the reference_audio folder
2. **Rate each aspect** using the scale below
3. **Mark any specific issues** in the notes section
4. **Provide overall feedback** at the end

### Rating Scale

- **5 - Excellent:** Perfect or near-perfect pronunciation, sounds natural
- **4 - Good:** Minor issues but generally correct and understandable
- **3 - Acceptable:** Some noticeable issues but still intelligible
- **2 - Poor:** Significant pronunciation errors, difficult to understand
- **1 - Unacceptable:** Completely incorrect or unintelligible
